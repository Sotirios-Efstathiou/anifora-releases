Read me first

Anifora is indoor cycling for the Wahoo KICKR CORE with Zwift Cog: ERG workouts, real roads from your own GPX files ridden in 3D, and virtual gears. Everything stays on your Mac.

You have an early version. This page is all you need for the first week. It works offline, and you can print it.

Install

You need a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later.

  1. Open the disk image you downloaded.
  2. Drag Anifora onto the Applications folder in the same window.
  3. Eject the disk image: click the eject button next to it in the Finder sidebar.
  4. Open Anifora from your Applications folder.

If you open Anifora straight from the disk image or from your Downloads folder, it asks Move Anifora to your Applications folder? Choose Move to Applications. Anifora can only update itself from there. Your rides and settings are not affected.

The first time you open this beta

This version is not signed by Apple yet, so macOS stops it once. You allow it in System Settings:

  1. Open Anifora. macOS says it could not verify that “Anifora” is free of malware. Click Done, not Move to Trash.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security. It says “Anifora” was blocked to protect your Mac. Click Open Anyway.
  3. macOS asks once more, and still advises against it. Click Open Anyway in that window too, and confirm with your password (or Touch ID) if asked. Anifora opens.

You do this once for each copy you install. The next versions will be signed by Apple, and this step goes away. Until then, macOS may ask for Bluetooth again after an update, and possibly for the Documents folder. Answer the same way you did the first time.

On a Mac that has never run Anifora, it opens on a welcome screen. It says where your data will live, that nothing is uploaded, how updates work, and that you ride at your own risk. Continue sets off the Documents question below. Then Anifora asks who rides (name, weight, height, FTP), looks for your trainer, and offers to import a GPX file. You can skip that last step with Later.

Permissions, and why

macOS asks two questions. Anifora asks for nothing else.

  • Documents folder. Anifora keeps your rides, roads and workouts in a folder called TrainerApp in your Documents folder. The welcome screen says so first, then macOS asks. Click OK (or Allow, on newer versions of macOS), or rides cannot be saved.
  • Bluetooth. Anifora talks to your trainer, power meter and heart-rate strap over Bluetooth. macOS asks when you first open Devices. Click Allow.

If you clicked Don’t Allow, it is not final. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, then:

  • for Bluetooth: Bluetooth, and switch Anifora on;
  • for Documents: Files & Folders, then Anifora, and switch on Documents Folder.

Then open Anifora again. Until you do, it tells you: Devices says “Bluetooth permission denied”, and a message at the top of the window says “Anifora cannot save” into the folder.

Try the demo trainer first

The demo trainer is a simulated KICKR. It makes up power, cadence and heart rate, so you can try every part of Anifora before you connect the real bike. Start here.

  1. Click Devices at the top of the window.
  2. With no trainer found, it offers No bike here? Ride with the demo trainer. Click it. Or switch on Demo trainer further down the same sheet.
  3. Devices says Demo trainer on — ready to ride. Click Ride.
  4. Choose a free ride, a workout or a road, and press Start ride or Start workout.

The switch Rider is pedalling in Devices stops the simulated legs, so you can see the ride pause itself. Demo rides are saved with DEMO_ at the start of the file name. They never change your profile, and Home does not count them in your week.

Your KICKR CORE

  • It has no battery. Plug it into mains power, and turn the cranks to wake it. Keep it within a few metres of the Mac.
  • Only one app can control the trainer at a time. Close Zwift and the Wahoo app, on this Mac and on your phone or tablet, or switch the phone’s Bluetooth off.
  • Open Devices and click Connect beside your KICKR CORE. You do this once. Anifora remembers it and connects by itself next time. Disconnect forgets it again.
  • When all is well, Devices says KICKR CORE … is connected and ready.

If something is wrong, Anifora says so before you ride, in red above the window and in Devices. What the messages mean:

The trainer will not take orders from Anifora
Another app is almost certainly holding it: Zwift, the Wahoo app or another trainer app. Close it, then press Disconnect and Connect in Devices. Until then the resistance will not change.
… cannot be controlled over Bluetooth
The trainer offers no FTMS, the standard Anifora sets resistance with. Update its firmware in the Wahoo app, then connect again.
… cannot hold a wattage
The trainer offers no target power (ERG), which every workout relies on. Update its firmware in the Wahoo app, then connect again.
The trainer sends power but no cadence
Anifora needs cadence to know you are pedalling and to turn the virtual gears. Turn the cranks a few times. If it stays at 0, update the firmware in the Wahoo app. A power meter that reports cadence also works.
This version is made for the Wahoo KICKR CORE with Zwift Cog
A grey notice, not an error. You connected a different trainer. ERG over Bluetooth may well work, but nothing else has been tested.

Anifora does not use the Zwift Click. You shift with the keys, the gear panel, or auto shift.

Riding

Home is the front page. Free ride is gears and no plan. Workouts are held in ERG. My roads holds the roads you bring. In Your ride at the top you can load a workout and a road together. Then go to the Ride page and press Start.

Anifora comes without climbs. Every road is a GPX file of yours: a Komoot tour, a Strava or RideWithGPS route, or a ride you recorded. Drop it on My roads, or click Import GPX…. The file stays on your Mac.

Your KICKR has one cog, so Anifora has the gears. Shift with ← and → or on the gear panel. Auto shift can do it for you, by cadence, by power, or by what the climb needs next. Its Rules button shows how.

← →
Easier or harder gear. On a road, and in a workout’s free blocks.
↑ ↓
How you sit: ↑ more relaxed, ↓ lower and faster.
⌘↑ ⌘↓
Harder or easier: a workout by 1 %, a free ride’s gradient by 0.5 %, or its target by 5 W. Add ⇧ for 5 %, 1 % or 10 W.
⌘← ⌘→
In a workout: this block again, or on to the next.
L
Mark a lap.
⇧⌘C ⌘Z
In 3D: change the camera, and how close it is. Esc brings it back to the rider after you have looked around with the mouse.
⌘1 … ⌘7
Home, Ride, Workouts, Climbs, My roads, Activities, Progress. ⌘[ goes back to Home. ⌘, opens Settings.

Stop pedalling and the ride pauses itself and takes the load off: at once in a workout, and on a road once the bike has rolled to a stop. Pause, Resume and Stop are on the ride screen. Stop ends the ride and saves it.

Quitting during a ride. ⌘Q asks End the ride and quit? Keep Riding is the default button, so a reflex Return does no harm. End Ride and Quit saves the ride and lets the trainer go first. Logging out or shutting down saves the ride without asking. If Anifora stops some other way, such as a crash, the ride so far is already on disk. Anifora offers it back the next time you open it: An unfinished ride was recovered, with Keep it or Discard.

Your data

  • Where. ~/Documents/TrainerApp holds your rides, roads (with each GPX file you imported, untouched), workouts and logs. Rider profiles and your stars for roads are in ~/Library/Application Support/TrainerApp. They are ordinary files you can open and copy.
  • Backup. Time Machine covers both. Otherwise, quit Anifora and copy those two folders somewhere safe. In Finder, hold ⌥ while you open the Go menu to see Library.
  • iCloud. If iCloud Drive keeps your Desktop and Documents in sync, the TrainerApp folder is synced with it. That is your Mac’s setting, not Anifora’s. With Optimize Mac Storage on, macOS may keep some of your rides and roads only in iCloud; Anifora then opens anyway and shows Getting your files from iCloud while they come down. To keep them on this Mac, right-click the TrainerApp folder in Documents and choose Keep Downloaded.
  • Strava or Garmin Connect. Every ride is saved as a .fit file and a .csv next to its other files. Open Activities, click the ride, then click Files. Finder shows the two files. Upload the .fit as you would any ride file: Upload activity on Strava, Import Data in Garmin Connect. The .csv has every second as the sensors reported it. Anifora does not smooth it.
  • Nothing leaves your Mac unless you send it. There is no account and no analytics.

Updates

When Anifora opens it looks for a new version. If there is one, a card says Updating to Anifora …: it downloads it, checks its signature, closes, installs it and opens again, in about half a minute. In a hurry? Skip This Time, and it installs when you quit instead. With no internet the card goes after three seconds and Anifora opens as usual.

It also looks once a day while it is open. An update found then installs when you quit; a banner says so: Anifora … is ready, with Restart Now. Your rides and settings carry over.

Never during a ride: no check, no notice, no restart. An important fix is asked about once, when you are off the bike.

To look right now, choose Anifora → Check for Updates… in the menu bar. To switch the looking off, at opening and daily, open Settings (⌘,).

Safety

  • Ride at your own risk. Anifora is training software, not a medical device, and nothing it shows is medical advice. Zones, training load and heart-rate figures are estimates. Check with a doctor before training hard, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or tightness in your chest.
  • ERG sets your trainer’s resistance for you. It lets go when you stop pedalling or stop the ride. Set up and use the trainer as Wahoo says.

The first week

You are the first people to start Anifora on Macs that have never seen it. Please try these, in any order:

  • The first run: the welcome, the rider, Devices, and a ride on the demo trainer.
  • A 20-minute free ride on your KICKR CORE. Shift gears with the arrow keys.
  • One of the starter workouts.
  • Import a GPX of a road you know, and ride part of it.
  • Quit during a ride (⌘Q) and see what it asks.
  • Find one ride’s .fit file in Activities, then Files, and upload it.

What to send back

  • Your trainer model, and whether cadence showed up.
  • Your Mac’s model, memory and macOS version (Apple menu, About This Mac).
  • Anything that felt wrong, with Help → Report a Problem….

Start a feedback email with these questions already in it.

Report a problem

Choose Help → Report a Problem…. Anifora collects its version, your Mac’s model and macOS version, its own log, and any crash reports, with your user name taken out. It never includes your rides or heart rate. Then it opens an email to Sotirios.Efstathiou@gmail.com with that attached, and you press Send.

If Mail is not set up, Anifora shows the file in Finder and copies the address. Attach the file to an email from whatever you use.
