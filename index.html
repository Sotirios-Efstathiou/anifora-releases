Private beta

Anifora is indoor cycling for the Wahoo KICKR CORE with Zwift Cog: ERG workouts, real roads from your own GPX files ridden in 3D, and virtual gears for the one-cog trainer. Everything you ride and record stays on your Mac.

This is an early version, shared with a few friends to try before anyone else. Please keep the link to yourself while it is a beta.

One extra step the first time

This beta is not signed by Apple yet, so macOS stops it the first time you open it. You allow it once in System Settings. The three steps are in the guide, and the same page is inside the disk image as “Read Me First”. Later versions will be signed, and this step goes away.

What you need

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later), running macOS 15 or later.
  • A Wahoo KICKR CORE with the Zwift Cog. This is the only trainer the beta is made and tested for.
  • Optional: a Bluetooth heart-rate strap, and a Bluetooth power meter.

No bike to hand? The built-in demo trainer rides everything with simulated power, cadence and heart rate.

What it does

  • Structured workouts held in ERG: .zwo, .erg, .mrc and TrainerDay files, plus a few starters and a builder.
  • Any road you bring as a GPX file, ridden in 3D with its real climbs and descents.
  • Virtual gears, by hand or by auto shift, because the Zwift Cog is a single sprocket.
  • Every ride saved on your Mac as a FIT file and a CSV. No account, no analytics.
